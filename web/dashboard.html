PhD Research Metrics

Tracking output for Psychology PhD students over a rolling six-year window (2020–2026). Cohort and area come from the Master Directory; metrics are refreshed daily and written to the PubWatch folder in Drive.

Coverage is partial, and the gaps are not random. Figures come from Crossref and count only work co-authored with the student’s listed advisor — Crossref has no author identifiers, so advisor co-authorship is the sole reliable way to tell a student from a namesake. Two consequences worth holding onto:

1. Independent work is invisible. A paper published without the advisor is not counted. Every number here is a floor.
2. Conference proceedings are largely missing. CogSci and similar venues are mostly not DOI-registered, so Crossref cannot see them. This undercounts Cognition and Developmental students specifically, relative to areas that publish in journals. In the pilot, a student who is first author on a CogSci paper showed zero here.

So a low number may mean low output, or it may mean the student publishes somewhere this method cannot reach. Do not read a zero as a finding.
Read the ranking with its limits in view. A composite score is only as good as its coverage, and coverage here is uneven. Citation counts favour senior cohorts mechanically, and accrue several times faster in Neuroscience than in Developmental work, so the within-cohort percentile is the more defensible column. Students below the coverage threshold are listed separately rather than ranked low by default. Nothing here measures teaching, mentoring, service, methods work, or the quality of a dissertation in progress.
